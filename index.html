The Great Gatsby

The Great Gatsby is a 20th Century classic set in the summer of 1922 in Long Island, New York City. Told by the first-person narrator, Nick Carraway, a Yale University graduate, the story follows Nick's neighbor, the elusive millionaire Jay Gatsby who hosts lavish and elaborate parties at his mansion in Long Island.

Will Gatsby's deep and undying love for his former flame, the beautiful, yet unattainable Daisy Buchanan lead him down a winding road to ruin or will his hot pursuit of the American Dream lead to his happily ever after?
